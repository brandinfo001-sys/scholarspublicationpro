01Research & Manuscript Development

Build a Stronger Scholarly Foundation

For projects that are still being structured, condensed, reorganized or developed into a journal-ready manuscript.

Research Planning & Framing

Clarify research objectives, question structure, study narrative and alignment between aims, methods and conclusions.

Manuscript Development

Support section planning, argument flow, literature positioning and coherent development from early draft to complete manuscript structure.

Thesis-to-Article Support

Condense a thesis or dissertation into a focused journal-article format while preserving the research contribution and appropriate authorship responsibility.

Research Paper Publication Support

Structured guidance for authors preparing original research articles, reviews, case reports and related scholarly outputs.

02Editing & Quality Enhancement

Improve Clarity Without Losing the Author’s Meaning

Editorial support focuses on communication quality, consistency and scholarly presentation.

Manuscript Editing / Academic & Scientific Editing

Improve academic style, sentence clarity, terminology consistency, transitions and discipline-appropriate presentation.

Language & Readability Review

Correct grammar and awkward phrasing while maintaining the intended technical meaning and author voice.

Plagiarism / Originality & Similarity Review

Identify passages that may need clearer attribution, citation or rewriting and flag potential similarity concerns for author review. Where a similarity-checking tool is used, results are interpreted rather than treated as an automatic plagiarism verdict.

References, Tables & Figures Review

Check consistency, journal conventions and presentation issues that can weaken a submission package.

03Journal Strategy & Preparation

Prepare for the Right Journal, Not Just Any Journal

Journal fit is assessed through topic, scope, readership, article type, indexing preferences and current submission requirements.

Journal Selection & Matching

Shortlist appropriate journals and explain the fit considerations relevant to the manuscript.

Pre-Submission Support

Review the manuscript and submission package before it reaches an editor’s desk.

Manuscript Formatting

Align structure, headings, references, tables, figures, word limits and other requirements with target-journal instructions.

Publication Guidance

Help authors understand practical publication routes, journal requirements and the steps that follow submission.

04Submission Support

Reduce Preventable Submission Problems

Prepare the manuscript and supporting materials so the submission is organized and consistent.

Journal Publication Assistance

Coordinate the practical preparation stages leading from a manuscript toward submission.

Submission Documentation

Support cover letters, highlights, declarations and other journal-requested materials where applicable.

Final Compliance Check

Review file completeness, journal formatting, references and unresolved manuscript queries before submission.

Submission Guidance

Explain journal portal requirements and help authors organize the information they need for submission.

05Peer Review & Resubmission

Respond to Editorial Feedback With a Clear Revision Plan

Reviewer comments are organized into practical actions so revisions and responses remain consistent.

Peer-Review Support

Interpret editor and reviewer comments, identify required revisions and create an organized revision plan.

Reviewer Comment Assistance

Prepare clear point-by-point responses and ensure manuscript changes match the stated response.

Resubmission Assistance

Check the revised manuscript and response package before resubmission to the same journal.

Alternative Journal Rematching

When a manuscript needs a new target, reassess fit and adapt the preparation package for a more suitable journal.
