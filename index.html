Trust & Research Integrity

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions Researchers Often Ask

Have a specific manuscript or reviewer decision? Send the details for a free initial consultation.

Talk to an Expert
Do you only edit completed papers?

No. Support can begin with research framing, manuscript planning or thesis-to-article development, and continue through editing, journal selection and submission preparation.

Can you guarantee publication?

No. Journal acceptance and publication decisions belong to independent editors and publishers. We support preparation, compliance and revision strategy.

Can you help identify Scopus-indexed journals?

We can support journal research based on your topic and indexing preferences. Indexing status, scope and policies should always be verified from current official sources before submission.

Can you help with reviewer comments?

Yes. Support can include reviewer-comment mapping, revision planning, response-letter preparation and final consistency checks before resubmission.

Do you support all subject areas?

We support projects across major academic disciplines and interdisciplinary topics. The exact scope depends on the research area and the type of specialist review required.
