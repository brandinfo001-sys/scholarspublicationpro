Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

1. Information we receive

When you contact Scholars Publication Pro, you may provide your name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, research field, manuscript details and any files you choose to send separately by email or messaging service.

2. How information is used

We use the information you provide to respond to enquiries, understand requested publication-support services, communicate about your project and provide the support you request.

3. Website forms

The forms on this static website prepare an email in your own email application. Form content is not uploaded by the website itself. Manuscript files are sent only when you choose to attach and send them through your email service.

4. Third-party services

If you contact us through WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, Threads or email, those services process information under their own privacy terms. The website also uses remotely hosted web fonts.

5. Manuscripts and confidential material

Only send information required for the requested service. Authors should remove unnecessary sensitive, personal or confidential data before sharing files. If a project requires specific confidentiality arrangements, agree them before sending sensitive project material.

6. Data retention

Project communications may be retained for as long as reasonably needed to manage the enquiry or provide the requested service, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

7. Your choices

You may contact us to ask about information you have provided or to request correction or deletion where applicable.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, email info.scholarspublicationpro@gmail.com.
